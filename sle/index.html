    Questions

    What people usually ask.

    What is it?

    Lifetime life insurance with no investment, that also pays you guaranteed cash for life.

    What do I get while I’m alive?

    Guaranteed cash every 2 years for life, starting at the end of year 6. Take it, or leave it in the plan to grow. Dividends can also be taken as cash or used to pay premiums (not guaranteed).

    What does my family get?

    Double your coverage amount, from day 1 until age 100.

    How do I pay?

    Regularly, or finish in 5 or 10 years. Yearly, every 6 months, every 3 months, or monthly through auto-debit after your first quarterly payment.

    How is it different from Smarter Life Classic?

    Both give 2× life cover until 100 with no market risk. Elite adds guaranteed cash every 2 years for life.
