VUL · Insurance with an investment fund

Sun MaxiLink Prime

VUL · Insurance + Investment · Life insurance that also grows your money.

Pay for at least 10 years · life cover of at least 2× your coverage · Minimum coverage: ₱350,000

What is it?
Life insurance and an investment in one plan. Part of what you pay protects your family, and the rest goes into a fund that can grow over time.
How do I pay?
You pay for at least 10 years. You can also add extra money (top-ups) anytime to grow your fund faster.
What does my family get?
If you pass away, your family receives at least 2× your coverage amount, plus whatever is in your fund.
What do I get while I’m alive?
A fund you can use later for retirement, your kids’ education or a big life goal.
Where does my money go?
You choose from a range of investment funds, from safer ones to more aggressive ones, depending on how much ups and downs you’re okay with. Professional fund managers handle the investing for you.
What if I need money?
You can withdraw part of your fund for emergencies.
Can I add more protection?
Yes. You can add riders for accidents, disability and illnesses on top of the basic plan.
What should I watch out for?
The fund isn’t guaranteed. It moves with the market, and if it runs low you may need to keep paying past 10 years. It works best if you keep it for 15 to 20 years or more.

Best for: Long-term investors, Want protection and growth, Can commit to the full term, Okay with market ups and downs.

Sun Life EasyLink Protect 10

VUL · Guaranteed Acceptance · Coverage even with an existing health condition.

Pay for 5 or 10 years · covered up to age 100 · Minimum coverage: ₱375,000

What is it?
Life insurance with an investment that anyone can apply for. No medical exam, and acceptance is guaranteed within the insurer’s limits.
How do I pay?
For 5 or 10 years, yearly, every 6 months, every 3 months or monthly. If money gets tight, you can pause payments (a premium holiday) while your fund covers the charges.
What does my family get?
Life cover until age 100 of at least 5× your yearly payment. In the first 2 years, if death is from illness, your family receives the fund value plus charges paid. Accidents are covered in full from day 1.
What do I get while I’m alive?
Three bonuses for staying on track: up to 25% of your yearly payment added at the end of your paying years; a portion of charges returned from year 10 and every 5 years after; and a 1% loyalty bonus from year 10 and every 2 years after (not guaranteed).
Where does my money go?
You choose from a range of investment funds, from safer ones to more aggressive ones, depending on how much ups and downs you’re okay with. Professional fund managers handle the investing for you. You can switch funds for free up to 4 times a year.
What if I need money?
You can withdraw part of your fund, but withdrawals or pausing payments may cost you your bonuses.
What should I watch out for?
It’s life insurance only, with no health or illness benefits. The 5 or 10 years of paying isn’t guaranteed; if the fund runs low, you may need to pay more.

Best for: Have a medical condition, Put off insurance because of the process, Small yearly budget, Want lifetime coverage.

Sun LifeAssure

Term + Critical Illness · The most affordable way to get protected.

Renews every 5 years · covered up to age 70 · Minimum coverage: ₱1,000,000

What is it?
Simple, affordable protection. It pays you cash if you get seriously sick, or pays your family if you pass away.
How do I pay?
Yearly or in smaller installments. The plan renews every 5 years up to age 70, and the price goes up with your age at each renewal.
What if I get sick?
You receive your full coverage amount as a lump sum if diagnosed with any of 32 critical illnesses, like heart attack, stroke, cancer, kidney failure or Alzheimer’s.
What about surgeries?
It also pays if you need any of 4 major surgeries: an organ transplant, heart bypass, aorta surgery or heart valve replacement.
How can I use the money?
However you want: treatment, recovery, alternative medicine, or simply things you’ve always wanted to do.
What does my family get?
If you pass away from something other than a covered illness, your family receives your coverage amount.
Can I add more protection?
Yes. You can add riders for accidents and disability.
What should I watch out for?
It pays once, either for the illness or for death, and then the plan ends. Nothing comes back if you stay healthy. A diagnosis in the first 90 days isn’t covered, and pre-existing conditions may be excluded.

Best for: Tight budget, First-time insurance buyers, Already have an HMO, Breadwinners.

Traditional · Health

Cash for illness and hospital bills

Sun Fit and Well Advantage 10

Comprehensive Health · 10-Pay · The most comprehensive health protection.

Pay for 10 years · covered up to age 100 · Minimum coverage: ₱300,000

What is it?
Complete health protection that pays you cash from the moment you’re diagnosed, through treatment and recovery. Your HMO pays the hospital; this pays you.
Which version should I get?
There are 3. Fit and Well covers illnesses. Fit and Well Plus adds cash back from age 65. Fit and Well Advantage, my best seller, adds both plus cash for hospital stays, follow-up check-ups, home recovery and palliative care.
How do I pay?
A fixed amount for 10, 15 or 20 years, yearly, every 6 months, every 3 months or monthly. Then you’re covered until age 100.
What if I get sick?
It covers 114 conditions: 64 major illnesses, 34 minor ones and 16 specific cancers. A minor illness pays part of your coverage, and you can still claim later for a major one.
What if it’s cancer?
You get an extra 50% of your coverage on first diagnosis of a covered cancer. On Advantage, the hospital, home recovery and palliative care benefits are also 50% higher.
What do I get while I’m alive?
From age 65, 5% of your coverage every year for 8 years, even if you never claim (Plus and Advantage). You also get a special bonus when you finish paying, then yearly dividends you can withdraw or leave to grow (not guaranteed).
What does my family get?
Your coverage amount, until age 100, minus anything already paid out for minor illnesses or cash back.
Real example
Parents insured their newborn for ₱1M, paying 15 years. He was hospitalized for dengue at 5, had kidney disease at 50, got ₱400K cash back from 65 to 72, and had kidney failure at 73. Total paid: ₱389,850. Total received: ₱1,023,750, plus dividends.
Anything else?
Access to a wellness program, with health info, fitness events and nutrition experts.
What should I watch out for?
The illness has to be one of the covered conditions in the policy, and pre-existing conditions may be excluded. Dividends and the special bonus are not guaranteed.

Best for: Working adults, Already have an HMO, Want big medical coverage, Want to finish paying early.

Sun First Aid

Hospital Income Plan · First Aid or First Aid Plus · Daily cash for every day you’re in the hospital.

Regular pay · First Aid Plus gives up to 75% back at year 10

What is it?
A hospital income plan. It gives you a fixed amount of cash for every day you’re confined in the hospital.
What does it cover?
Hospital confinement, surgery, long hospital stays and ICU admission. The daily amount depends on the plan level you pick.
Why do I need it if I have an HMO?
Your HMO pays the hospital. This pays you, for the income you lose while you’re out and the extra costs like transport, food and a bantay.
What does my family get?
If you pass away, your family receives the total premiums you’ve paid. If it’s from an accident, they receive double.
What if I can’t work anymore?
If you become totally disabled, you no longer need to pay premiums, and your coverage continues.
First Aid vs First Aid Plus?
Same hospital cash. First Aid Plus adds a money-back feature: up to 75% of your total premiums back on the 10th policy anniversary, if you outlive the protection period.
What should I watch out for?
Only First Aid Plus gives money back. The regular First Aid is pure protection.

Best for: Breadwinners who can’t afford days off, Freelancers with no paid sick leave, Want cash on top of an HMO, Small budget.

Common questions

Which insurance plan is right for me?

It depends on what you want to protect. Enter your age and pick what’s on your mind on this page, like protecting your family, big hospital bills, your kids’ schooling or retirement, and you’ll see the plans that fit, best match first.

How much does life insurance cost per month in the Philippines?

It depends on your age, the plan and the coverage amount. On this page, a 35-year-old starts from about ₱650 a month, or about ₱21 a day, at minimum coverage. Enter your age above to see an estimate for each plan.

How much life insurance coverage do I need?

A simple rule of thumb is enough to replace your income for 3 years, plus 2 more years for each person who depends on you, up to 10 years, minus any coverage you already have. For illness cover, aim for about 3 years of income. Use the “How much coverage do I need?” check on this page to get your number in 10 seconds.

VUL or traditional insurance: which is better?

Neither is better for everyone. A VUL gives you life insurance plus an investment fund that can grow, but the fund value isn’t guaranteed. Traditional plans give fixed, guaranteed benefits with no market ups and downs. Use the VUL or Traditional switch on this page to see only the type you prefer.

Can I get insurance for my senior parents?

Yes. SUN Senior Care is made for people in their senior years and pays cash for 17 age-related illnesses like stroke and heart attack. Sun Life EasyLink Protect needs no medical exam, which helps if your parent already has a health condition.

Is there insurance for OFWs?

Yes. Sun Life OFW Health Protect gives health and life protection that covers you while working abroad, with yearly check-up benefits. You need to apply while you’re in the Philippines.

Is there insurance with no medical exam?

Yes. Sun Life EasyLink Protect offers guaranteed acceptance with no medical exam, within the insurer’s limits. Sun Life Save and Protect, Sun MaxiLink One and Sun Life Wealth Prosper 7 can also be applied for without a medical exam, within the insurer’s limits.

What is a VUL?

A VUL (variable universal life) is life insurance and an investment in one plan. Part of what you pay protects your family and the rest goes into a fund that can grow. Sun MaxiLink Prime, MaxiLink 100, MaxiLink Bright, MaxiLink One, FlexiLink and Sun Life EasyLink Protect are VULs.
