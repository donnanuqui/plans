    Questions

    What people usually ask.

    Which version should I get?

    There are 3. Fit and Well covers illnesses. Fit and Well Plus adds cash back from age 65. Fit and Well Advantage, my best seller, adds both plus cash for hospital stays, follow-up check-ups, home recovery and palliative care.

    How do I pay?

    A fixed amount for 10, 15 or 20 years, yearly, every 6 months, every 3 months or monthly. Then you’re covered until age 100. The estimate on this page is for 10 years.

    What if I get a minor illness first?

    A minor illness pays part of your coverage, and you can still claim later for a major one.

    What if it’s cancer?

    You get an extra 50% of your coverage on first diagnosis of a covered cancer. On Advantage, the hospital, home recovery and palliative care benefits are also 50% higher.

    What if I never get sick?

    From age 65, you get 5% of your coverage every year for 8 years. You also get a special bonus when you finish paying, then yearly dividends you can withdraw or leave to grow (not guaranteed).

    What does my family get?

    Your coverage amount, until age 100, minus anything already paid out for minor illnesses or cash back.
